# Coding Instructions

## Task

Read the lyrics for each song and determine whether the song is **political**.

## What counts as political?

"Political" is intentionally not defined here. It is up to you as the coder to decide what you understand as a political or politics-related song. You may rely on your own background knowledge if you already know a song or its context. However, you are not allowed to google songs or ask ChatGPT about them, meaning no online research into the background or context of a song unless you happen to already know it. You also should not discuss it with other people, especially other coders, or look it up on Soptify to listen to it.

## Coding fields

- **Is political?** -- Yes or No. Choose Yes if in your opinion the song engages with political topics.
- **Brief justification** -- One or two sentences explaining why you classified the song this way. Reference specific lyrics if helpful.
- **Confidence** -- A number from 0.0 (completely uncertain) to 1.0 (completely certain). Move the slider to adjust.

## Tips

- Read the full lyrics before coding.
- Use **Prev** / **Next** to navigate between songs. Use **Next uncoded** to jump to the next song you haven't coded yet.
- Coded items show a checkmark in the dropdown.
- Your work is saved per entry. You can revisit and change your coding at any time.
- Use **Save & Next** to save and immediately advance to the next uncoded song.
- Use **Export CSV** to download all codings (yours and other coders') for analysis.
